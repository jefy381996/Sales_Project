
# Sales Performance Analytics Project

## Business Problem
This project analyzes sales performance data for last 3 months, 4 categories, random Sample of users and sample of products to identify revenue drivers, profitability patterns, and time-based trends to support business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Identify top and low performing products and categories
- Analyze revenue and profit trends over time
- Compare weekday vs weekend performance
- Provide actionable insights for sales strategy

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, SQLAlchemy, Matplotlib)
- Docker Container for PostgreSQL database
- PgAdmin for Database management
- PostgreSQL (SQL)
- Jupyter Notebook
- Power BI (dashboard specification included)

## Project Structure
- data/
  - raw/
  - cleaned/
- sql/
- notebook/
- insights/
- report/

## Key Insights (Summary)
- Top 20% of products generate majority of revenue
- Profit margins vary significantly by category
- Weekday sales are stronger than weekend sales
- Revenue is concentrated in a small number of categories
- 18% Products drive high revenue but low profit
- Certain Products Remained low selling in all months
- 26% Products Remained High selling in all months

## Dashboard
A Power BI dashboard is designed to visualize KPIs, trends, Products and category performance.
