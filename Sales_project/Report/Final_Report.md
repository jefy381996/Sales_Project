
# Sales Performance Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides an analysis of sales data to help stakeholders understand revenue, product, category and profit drivers.

## Business Questions
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products and categories generate low revenue?
- Does sale, product pattern change with months?
- Is retail price a factor for low selling products?
- How does profitability vary over time?
- Are weekends or weekdays more profitable?

## Methodology
Data was collected with the help of data collection team, some data was gathered from data entry operators, Stakeholders and data records, data was cleaned using Python and Pandas, and analyzed using SQL queries and Pandas.

## Key Findings
- Top 20% of products generate majority of revenue
- Profit margins vary significantly by category
- Weekday sales are stronger than weekend sales
- Revenue is concentrated in a small number of categories
- 18% Products drive high revenue but low profit
- Certain Products Remained low selling in all months
- 26% Products Remained High selling in all months

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing on high-margin categories
- Expand Grocery SKUs
- Highlight High margin, low volume products
- Price is weakly correlated with quantity sold
- Re-evaluate pricing of low-profit high-volume products
- Promote low-selling products with discounts/Bundles with popular
- Increase inventory for high-demand products
- Optimize staffing for weekday peaks

## Limitations
- Dataset limited to historical sales
- Only a random sample of customers was choosen
- Only few Random products were choosen from categories
- Only 4 categories were choosen
- No customer demographic data
